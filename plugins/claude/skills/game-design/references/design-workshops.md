# Choose a design workshop

Pick the question closest to what the creator wants to decide. Work through it with their scene, rules or prototype. If they already asked for a concrete edit, give them the edit in this turn.

## When the player does something unexpected

| What is happening in the game? | Exercise to try |
|---|---|
| Players can explain the rule afterwards but miss it in play. | [Find the cue they had before acting](workshops/diagnose-player-decisions.md). Then [reconstruct the attempt from their point of view](workshops/reconstruct-a-player-decision.md). |
| A player knows what to do but can't carry it out with their usual controls. | [Trace their input and feedback through the whole task](workshops/test-access-through-play.md). |
| Everyone finds the clue eventually. They still call the puzzle unfair. | [Try the first visit, a sensible wrong answer and the return route](workshops/test-adventure-clues-and-routes.md). |
| The jump works in the test room and feels wrong in the course. | [Compare what the player can see, when they can steer and where they land](workshops/shape-platform-movement.md). |
| The tutorial is easy, but the next room has to explain everything again. | [Build an unfamiliar situation that uses the same skill](workshops/teach-a-skill-through-play.md). |
| Players make it to the ending. You're unsure what they think changed. | [Trace the consequence they actually encountered](workshops/test-consequences-and-replay.md). |

## When the rules work once

| What would you like to learn about the next turn, run or visit? | Exercise to try |
|---|---|
| Can two people teach themselves this tabletop game? | [Play through a turn handoff and a disputed move](workshops/test-tabletop-rules.md). |
| Do generated rooms ask for different decisions? | [Compare reachable routes and successful shortcuts across runs](workshops/validate-generated-runs.md). |
| Is buying another upgrade still an interesting decision? | [Trace a first purchase and the next chance to spend](workshops/trace-an-economy-through-play.md). |
| Does what happened yesterday still matter to someone signing in today? | [Try two visits to the same place](workshops/prototype-a-persistent-world.md). |
| Do people have a reason to organize or to help someone new? | [Test the choices available to each member](workshops/test-a-shared-world-community.md). |
| One player rejoins halfway through and cannot follow the plan. | [Give the returning player a chance to recover the missing context](workshops/help-groups-rejoin-play.md). |
| A character choice changes a variable but feels identical either way. | [Make its meaning show up in a witness, a later action or a relationship](workshops/make-character-choices-matter.md). |

## When you have a scene to make

| What is on the creator's desk? | Where to start |
|---|---|
| A reveal that makes sense to its writer but surprises nobody | [Change whose eyes we see through](storycraft/references/viewpoint-and-reveals.md), or [move the beat that sets up the ending](storycraft/references/scene-structure-and-endings.md). |
| A draft that tells us what someone felt before we can feel it | [Revise the scene and its lines](storycraft/references/scene-and-line-revision.md). If the player controls the order, [try two ways to discover the same detail](storycraft/references/playable-scene-discovery.md). |
| Two paths on a floor plan | [Inspect the first consequential fork](maps/references/test-route-decisions.md), then [try navigating by a different cue](maps/references/sensory-wayfinding.md). |
| A level or its on-screen map | [Lay out a movement phrase](maps/references/platforming-rhythm.md), or [choose the symbols and connections a player needs at this scale](maps/references/map-symbols-and-scale.md). |
| A frame where an important object disappears | [Compose the view around its large shapes](art-direction/references/playable-composition.md) and [compare a palette across game states](art-direction/references/palette-and-state.md). |
| An art style that works in a single drawing | [Check the poses at play size](art-direction/references/readable-motion.md), then [cost the second asset](art-direction/references/2d-art-production.md). |
| An ability the player always casts immediately | [Try a cost that creates a real choice](spells/references/spell-costs-and-choices.md) or [let a second player answer it](spells/references/spell-response-windows.md). |
| A spell or continuous effect that hides what it does | [Find the edge that must read in a crowded frame](spells/references/overlapping-effect-readability.md), or [make a current's direction visible across a bend](spells/references/directional-currents.md). |

For a complete review, follow [one creator's question from observation through a proposed change](whole-game-review.md). For a change that could involve writing, layout or art, try [the closed cinema](closed-cinema.md). For a spell seen by two players, [watch a temporary bridge close](borrowed-step.md).
